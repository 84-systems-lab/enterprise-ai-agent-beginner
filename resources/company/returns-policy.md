# Everyday Office Supplies
## Returns and Damaged Goods Policy

Fictional teaching fixture version 1.0. Not legal or commercial advice.

## 1 Purpose
Customers need a clear process for reporting damage to delivered goods.
This policy describes the information needed to open a review.

## 2 Reporting damage
Report a damaged chair within 7 calendar days of delivery.
Provide the order number and clear photographs of the damage.
A support officer records the request. A refund is not automatic.

## 3 Review and response
The Returns team checks the delivery date and supporting photographs.
The team contacts the customer if important information is missing.
An authorised employee records the final decision in the support Case.
The assistant may explain this process. It cannot approve a refund.
