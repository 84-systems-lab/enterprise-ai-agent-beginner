# Damaged Chair Triage Procedure

Fictional teaching fixture version 1.0. General staff document.

## 1 Record the request
Open a support Case. Record the customer's order number and delivery date.
Attach the photographs supplied by the customer. Assign the Case to Returns.
Do not promise a replacement before the Returns team has reviewed it.

## 2 Missing information
If photographs or an order number are missing, ask for them.
Keep the Case open while the request is reviewed.
